## Non-negotiable design pillars

1. **C&C readability:** economy, production, power, base expansion, unit counters, defense, and battlefield control must be legible at a glance.
2. **Espionage consequence:** stealth, escalation, recovery, EW, information, identity, anti-war cost, and moral ambiguity must alter play rather than decorate it.
3. **Stealth-first, not stealth-only:** ordinary Metal Gear-style operations should teach **observe → identify → infiltrate/manipulate → recover/extract → escalate if chosen or forced**. Recon/infiltration is the default advisor bias; a turret or assault is never the universal opener. Quiet, nonlethal, recovery-heavy, and infrastructure-preserving play earns meaningful bonuses, but detection creates new gameplay rather than an automatic reload requirement.
4. **Earn the boss escalation:** opening operations may have no boss; early finales use elite humans, compact squads, Walker Gears, or conventional vehicles; experimental command platforms arrive later; giant strategic/Metal Gear-adjacent spectacle is late Act IV only. Human bosses stay human scale. David / Rotten Snake is a late-game relationship/command confrontation, never a tutorial-theater boss.
5. **Lore provenance is mandatory:** official chronology and released-game facts outrank supplemental/cut/fan material. Cut material can inspire unresolved bridges; fandom can index possibilities; neither silently becomes canon. Original 1987–1990 additions must preserve later hard-stop events and famous technologies.
6. **Buildings are griddy; troops are free:** authored terrain, structures, walls, bridges, deployables, and objectives obey logical cells/footprints. Units retain continuous real-time movement and use terrain/routes as costs and opportunities rather than being locked to lanes or tile hopping. The visual grid is contextual, not permanent wallpaper.
7. **Maps are systems, not backgrounds:** terrain type, lanes, cross-links, cover, concealment, elevation, water, hazards, patrol/search geometry, buildability, traversal links, control nodes, and objective dependencies must affect decisions. A visually attractive painted background is insufficient by itself.
8. **Visual progression is readable:** unit/building art must express role, footprint, adjacency, damage and meaningful upgrade state. A 2×2 building fills a 2×2 footprint; compatible neighbors connect; a level-3 asset does not look like level 1.
9. **Persistent command:** bases, veterans, casualties, infrastructure, research, and decisions persist through a theater.
10. **Two complete terminal routes:** a theater always reaches an explicit victory or an explicit defeat. A checkpoint is never visually or mechanically confused with game over. Build 2.0 may add multiple tactical approaches to an objective but must not fork this authority.
11. **Usability is not progression:** readable text, saves, touch parity, and core command tools are never unlocks.
12. **Close development; original presentation:** private development may use the active Eli/David storyline and era-appropriate Metal Gear lore, but do not copy official art, music, logos, dialogue, UI, maps, or likenesses. Broad-release adaptation is a separate future decision.
13. **Survivable learning curve:** Guided and Standard may rescue Forward Command once per operation, but the reserve is visible, finite, and never replaces repair, defense, stealth recovery, or a real defeat state.
14. **Economy lives on the map:** passive income keeps a run solvent, while larger gains require controlled territory, recovery/logistics, and infrastructure the enemy can disrupt. Build 2.0 adds an explicit low-signature versus high-signature forward-support tradeoff rather than assuming every operation should become a turret base.
15. **Construction is an informed commitment:** selecting a build card never spends GMP. The player first sees exact cost, time, footprint, power, supply, outputs, prerequisites, unlocks, and tradeoffs, then deliberately fabricates and places it.
16. **Commands survive complexity:** direct orders replace by default; Queue/Shift appends a visible, save-safe plan; formations create distinct slots; focus, guard, fallback, selection cycling, and contextual role actions stay available on touch and desktop. The new UI must compact these commands, not remove them.
17. **Now / Do / Plan UI hierarchy:** persistent HUD contains only what is needed for the next decision; contextual surfaces contain immediate actions for the selected thing; tactical map/build/deployment/R&D/intel screens contain deeper planning. A feature does not earn a permanent card merely because it exists.
18. **Proof before propagation:** GHOST LINE is the Build 2.0 vertical slice. Do not author 59 bespoke refoundation maps, bosses, or roster expansions until Ghost, Control, and Force approaches work end to end and the new world/UI/lore contracts are reusable.

## Definition of done for a gameplay slice

- Mouse, touch, and keyboard controls do not trigger accidental browser selection.
- Required text is readable at default settings on phone and desktop.
- The full mission-state graph remains covered by automated checks.
- Victory, defeat, checkpoint, save, and resume paths remain valid.
- If the mission fiction permits stealth, at least one real low-signature completion route exists and the adaptive advisor can recommend it without turning the recommendation into a hidden prerequisite.
- A recommended specialist being unavailable cannot strand mission progression if another legitimate route exists.
- Boss class/scale matches the fiction and campaign tier; Sable Crown has no Metal Gear-scale finale.
- Major lore additions carry provenance and preserve 1995/1999/2005 hard stops.
- Structures remain selectable across their full visible footprint and visually respect their declared footprint/connection/upgrade rules.
- Traversal devices, terrain, reveal state, and control/build authority are consumed by shared world/navigation state rather than being visual-only exceptions.
- Battlefield HUD obeys the Now / Do / Plan hierarchy and does not regress to permanent stat/card soup.
- Canonical records and the ordered backlog are reconciled before implementation is considered complete.
